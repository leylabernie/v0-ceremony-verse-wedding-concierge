Complete Guest Logistics & Gift Coordination Checklist
By Mini Patel | Updated October 10, 2026

Build one family guest list
[ ] Assign a household ID, guest count, home city, preferred departure airport and RSVP owner.
[ ] Record each family's room needs and intended dates; keep passport numbers and payment details out of shared trackers.
[ ] Ask guests privately about accessibility and dietary requirements; share only necessary details with the responsible vendor.
[ ] Separate invited, confirmed, room-booked and travel-booked statuses. Name one person who follows up.

Agree the room-block terms
[ ] Get the room category, occupancy basis, taxes, minimum stay and payment currency in writing.
[ ] Mark deposit, final payment, booking cut-off and cancellation deadlines in one calendar.
[ ] Confirm pickup, attrition, unused inventory and how individually booked rooms count toward concessions.
[ ] Ask exactly which earned rooms or credits apply, who receives them, and whether guest transfers or event access cost extra.
[ ] Identify who handles guest booking changes and after-hours travel problems. Planning support and supplier booking are separate scopes.

Coordinate arrivals and celebration days
[ ] Group arrival windows and assign a transfer contact; check luggage capacity and accessible vehicle requests.
[ ] Share an itinerary with local times, meeting points, attire, contact names and weather plans.
[ ] Name an owner for elder assistance, family photographs and moving guests between events.
[ ] Confirm baraat route and sound permissions, ceremony timing with the pandit, and event-space access in the written proposal.
[ ] Distinguish vegetarian, Jain and satvik requirements with the catering team; confirm what they can deliver in writing.

Coordinate outfits across countries
[ ] Create a person-by-event outfit matrix with size, colour, budget, measurements and shipping destination.
[ ] Choose readymade or custom-made for each person. Approve reference photos, fabric and the exact scope before payment.
[ ] Use one measurement method and an owner for missing measurements. Include shoe, dupatta and accessory needs.
[ ] Check progress, approve finished-piece photos or video, and allow time for local alterations.
[ ] Confirm production, shipping, customs and delivery buffers individually; a wedding date is not a delivery guarantee.

Plan gifts and welcome bags
[ ] Separate household gifts, gift sarees, wedding-party gifts, welcome bags and ceremonial items.
[ ] Set an all-in allowance per recipient including packing, delivery and any import costs.
[ ] Record recipient, quantity, approved item, source, purchase status and delivery owner.
[ ] Use a sample before bulk purchase; check personalisation spelling and avoid fragile or restricted items without checking the carrier's rules.
[ ] Confirm resort receiving, storage and room-delivery charges. Assign a handover count and keep spare bags separate.

Close the loop before departure
[ ] Reconcile the room list, transfer list, event attendance and gift distribution totals.
[ ] Send a final guest note with local contacts and what is included versus paid separately.
[ ] Keep supplier confirmations and changes together; assign the decision-maker for urgent issues.
[ ] Build a backup plan for delayed bags, missing outfits and weather changes.
[ ] After the wedding, reconcile earned concessions, unclaimed gifts and outstanding balances against the contract.
